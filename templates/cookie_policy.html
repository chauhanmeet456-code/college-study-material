Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. StudyHub uses only the storage needed to keep the service secure and, when you consent, remember an optional display preference.

Necessary cookies

Necessary cookies support authentication, signed sessions, CSRF protection, and security. They are always enabled because the site cannot provide protected account features safely without them. Never store passwords, OTPs, or sensitive personal information in cookies.

Optional preference storage

If you opt in, StudyHub may use browser storage to remember display preferences such as dark mode. This is optional and is not activated before consent. StudyHub does not set analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Consent and changing your choice

On your first visit, you can accept all optional preferences, reject them, or choose them individually in Cookie Settings. Your choice is kept in a signed, HttpOnly cookie for up to one year. You can change or withdraw it at any time using Cookie Preferences in the footer.

Browser controls and privacy

You can clear or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent login, sessions, and security checks from working. StudyHub aims to follow applicable privacy requirements, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework where applicable; this page is an explanation of the site's current storage practices, not legal advice.
